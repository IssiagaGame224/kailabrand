About us

Born in Cali, inspired by all of Colombia

Kaila Brand is a Colombian brand creating collectible postcards inspired by the landscapes, culture, people and identity of Colombia — with a special focus on Cali and the Colombian Pacific.

Our goal: to create a tangible way to remember, share and discover Colombia through beautifully designed postcards. Every card is an invitation to slow down, write and send — like before, but better.

Cali, the city that dances

From Cristo Rey overlooking the city to the salsa nights of Juanchito, the Cali collection is a vibrant tribute to caleño identity.

The story

The Pacific, an authentic Colombia

A region of exceptional cultural and natural richness, the Colombian Pacific is home to Afro-Colombian communities whose art, music and traditions deeply inspire Kaila Brand.

Humpback whales, black-sand beaches, mangroves and coconut palms: every card is an open window onto this preserved territory.

Our Story · Colombia

Every card tells a story

Behind every Kaila Brand design lies a place, a community, a memory. This section of the website is dedicated to the stories that inspire our collections.

Chapter 01

The colors of Colombia

From the alleys of Cartagena to the mountains of the Valle, the Colombian palette is our first source of inspiration. Every shade in our cards is drawn from the real landscape.

Chapter 02

The communities of the Pacific

The Pacific Collection pays tribute to Afro-Colombian communities, guardians of a living culture: currulao music, craftsmanship, traditional fishing and whale festivals.

Chapter 03

Salsa, a living heritage

Declared Intangible Cultural Heritage of Colombia, caleño salsa is far more than a dance: it is the language of the city. Our cards capture its energy and grace.

“We don't draw souvenirs. We draw Colombia as it lives, dances and breathes.”

— Kaila Brand
